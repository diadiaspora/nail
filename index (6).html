Our approach

What makes a Plume & Polish visit different?

Healthy first

Hospital-grade sterilized tools, gentle prep and no over-filing. Your natural nails and lashes stay strong between visits.

Made for your week

Pick a finish that fits your routine, from desk-friendly neutrals to full-glam length. Combine nails and lashes in a single booking.

Detail you can see

Every set is mapped to your nail bed or eye shape, then finished by hand. No two sets look the same, and that's the point.

About the studio

A small studio with a big love for the details.

Plume & Polish began with one table, one lamp and a belief that getting your nails and lashes done should feel like an exhale. Today we're a calm, bright boutique where every technician is licensed, trained in nail and lash health, and genuinely happy to see you.

Come in for a quick refresh, a full transformation, or a treat before a big day.

What it is

  • Clean, sterilized tools every visit
  • A real consultation before we start
  • Gentle on your natural nails and lashes
  • Nails and lashes in one booking

What it is not

  • A rushed assembly line
  • One look for everyone
  • Heavy sets that damage
  • Surprise charges at checkout
